Privacy Policy — Cricket Dynasty: T20 Manager

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Cricket Dynasty: T20 Manager ("the App") is a single-player sports management game. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Data We Collect

None. The App does not collect, store on any server, or transmit any personal or usage data off your device, in any form.

Locally stored game data. The App stores your career progress — team rosters, player stats, league standings, transfer history, and settings — directly on your device using Android's local app storage. This data never leaves your device, and we do not have access to it. Uninstalling the App, or using the in-app "Delete Career" option, permanently removes this data.

Account information. The App does not require account creation, login, or any personal identifying information (name, email, phone number) to play.

Analytics, advertising, and purchases. The App currently contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs of any kind, and no in-app purchases are active in this build.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or any data beyond what's described above. We do not sell or share user data with third parties, because none is collected.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Data Retention & Deletion

All save data lives entirely on your device. You can delete it at any time via the in-app "Delete Career" option, or by uninstalling the App.

Changes to This Policy

If the App's features change in the future (for example, if ads or in-app purchases are added), this policy will be updated accordingly, and the update date above will change.
