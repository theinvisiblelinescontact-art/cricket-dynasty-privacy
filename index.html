Privacy Policy — Cricket Dynasty: T20 Manager

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Cricket Dynasty: T20 Manager ("the App") is a single-player sports management game. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Data We Collect

The App itself does not collect, store on any server, or transmit any personal data off your device. The App uses two third-party Google SDKs — Google AdMob (advertising) and Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) — described in detail below. Those SDKs handle certain technical data on Google's own systems as part of showing ads and processing purchases; the App does not operate any server of its own and cannot see or access that data.

Locally stored game data. The App stores your career progress — team rosters, player stats, league standings, transfer history, and settings — directly on your device using Android's local app storage. This data never leaves your device, and we do not have access to it. Uninstalling the App, or using the in-app "Delete Career" option, permanently removes this data.

Account information. The App does not require account creation, login, or any personal identifying information (name, email, phone number) to play.

Advertising (Google AdMob). The App integrates the Google AdMob SDK to show optional rewarded video ads and occasional interstitial ads. AdMob uses your device's Android Advertising ID for ad personalization and measurement. If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App shows a consent choice — via Google's User Messaging Platform — before any personalized ad is served, letting you consent, decline, or manage detailed preferences by vendor and purpose. You can change that choice at any time from the App's Store screen ("Manage Ad Consent Choices"). The App is registered as general-audience, not directed at children, with Google AdMob. AdMob's own data collection and use is governed by Google's policies — see Google's Partner Sites policy and Google AdMob's data disclosure guidance for what Google itself does with this data; the App does not control Google's SDK-level data handling, only discloses that it is present.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing). The App offers optional in-app purchases (currency packs and one-time unlocks) processed entirely through Google Play's own billing system — not by any server or system this App controls. The App itself only stores a small, temporary local record confirming a purchase is being completed (product, amount, and Google's own purchase reference), so an interrupted purchase can be safely finished if the App closes mid-transaction. That record never leaves your device and is deleted automatically once the purchase finishes. Your payment details (card number, billing address, etc.) are handled entirely by Google Play and are never seen by the App.

Data We Do Not Collect

The App itself does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or location. This is separate from, and does not control, what Google's AdMob and Google Play Billing SDKs handle on their own behalf as described above — we disclose their presence and general purpose, but Google, not this App, is responsible for and controls that SDK-level data handling. We do not sell user data, and we do not share any data with third parties beyond what AdMob and Google Play Billing inherently require to function.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The App is deliberately registered as general-audience (not child-directed) with Google AdMob, and does not tag users as being under the age of consent by default — this is an explicit choice we made given the App's content and intended audience, not a default left unset.

Data Retention & Deletion

All save data lives entirely on your device. You can delete it at any time via the in-app "Delete Career" option, or by uninstalling the App. Since the App does not operate its own server and does not collect personal data itself, there is no separate account or server-side data to request deletion of beyond what's described above.

Changes to This Policy

If the App's features change further in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly, and the update date above will change.
